# Beyond the Baobab — two versions to compare

Hi! This folder contains **two versions of your website** so you can decide which direction you prefer. They are identical in design, photography and features — they differ only in the **messaging and the top of the page**.

## How to look at them

Double-click each file to open it in your web browser:

- **`version-a.html`** — *Original.* The hero opens on the film alone; the introduction leads with the method ("A safari planned from inside Botswana"); your track-record figures sit inside the "Why us" section.
- **`version-b.html`** — *Repositioned.* The hero carries a headline and a "Plan your safari" button on top of the film; the introduction leads with *your traveller* ("You get one first safari…"); there's a dedicated figures band high up (including "first citizen-owned lodge in the Delta"); testimonials show each guest's country and trip; the focus is tightened to Botswana.

Both are fully polished and ready to publish — the enquiry form works in both (it opens your email app pre-filled to Kristy), and both are set up correctly for Google.

> **Version B** is the stronger "market leader" positioning — it states your promise on sight and puts your proof up front. **Version A** is the calmer, more understated original. There's no wrong answer; it's a matter of the voice you want to lead with.

## Once you've chosen

1. Rename your chosen file to **`index.html`** (right-click → Rename).
2. That's the one you publish. See **`GUIDE.md`** for how to put it on GitHub and get it live — all by asking Claude in plain English.

## Keep the folder together

Both versions share the **`assets`** folder (the images and videos). Keep everything in this folder together, or the pictures won't show.

Any questions, just ask Claude — it knows this site inside out.
